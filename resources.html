Course Levels & Exams

German is learned in sequence: first A1, then A2, B1 and B2. The Goethe-Institut is the only institution recognised by the German government for language certification. We prepare students for Goethe and ÖSD exams.

FAQ

Are classes online?

Yes. We operate remotely and teach live on Zoom, because our faculty is half in Germany and half in Pakistan.

What is the difference between Normal Track and the faster courses?

Normal Track includes exam preparation, all exam material and extra vocabulary PDFs. The Crash Course has no exam material.

Group size?

Group classes have a minimum of 2 and a maximum of 4 students.
