DFA Kids · Book 4 · Activity

Der Regenbogen

Rainbow and Weather Words in German for Kids

  • Age3–7 years
  • LevelBeginner, no German needed
  • Pages100
  • Size8.5 × 11 in, black-and-white inside

Regen fällt, die Sonne lacht … and then a rainbow appears! This book follows Fridolin the fox and Hanna the bunny through a rainy day and teaches the weather words and the six rainbow colours in German.

You will find colouring and tracing pages for sun, cloud, rain, umbrella, wellies, puddle and more, a page for each rainbow colour, rainbow by number, a weekly weather chart, "What do I put on?", comics, mazes, a word search and cut-outs. The little rainbow poem from our video is in there too, with an English line under every German line.

Made to go with the DFA Kids rainbow video.
